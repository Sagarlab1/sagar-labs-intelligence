# Powered-Site Readiness Checklist

Lead magnet draft for Sagar Infrastructure Signal. Public template only; it is not an engineering, legal, investment or permitting opinion.

## How to use

For each gate, record the source, access date, authority, evidence state and the exact missing document. Do not convert an open gate into a numeric score.

Evidence states: `[VERIFIED]`, `[ESTIMATED]`, `[HYPOTHESIS]`, `[UNKNOWN]`, `[CLIENT_PROVIDED]`, `[CONFLICTED]`.

## Decision posture

- `ADVANCE`: every critical gate has adequate evidence for the defined decision.
- `CONDITION`: advance only against named evidence requests and owners.
- `PAUSE`: the next decision-changing proof is not yet available.
- `REDESIGN`: the current site or plan fails a material requirement.
- `DO NOT ADVANCE`: evidence contradicts the required posture or risk is unacceptable.

## Human review checkpoint

Before sharing externally, a human reviewer must confirm:

1. Every material claim has a traceable source.
2. Public signals are not presented as delivery, feasibility or customer results.
3. Unknowns and conflicts are visible.
4. The CTA asks for a decision or evidence request, not an unsupported commitment.

Suggested CTA: “Bring us a site, project or decision. We will define the evidence boundary and the next document worth requesting.”
